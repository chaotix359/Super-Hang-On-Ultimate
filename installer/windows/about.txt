SUPER HANG-ON ULTIMATE
Native Port + Mega Drive Original Mode Mod

This installer BUILDS the game on your computer from your own copy of the arcade ROM.

What you need:
  * The MAME ROM set "shangon.zip" (Super Hang-On sitdown/upright, unprotected).
    It is NOT included with this installer.
  * Optional: your Mega Drive ROM, "Super Hang-On (World) (En,Ja) (Rev A)", for Original Mode.
  * About 1.5 GB of free disk space while installing (most of it is temporary) and
    about 5-15 minutes. The build tools are removed again when the installer finishes.

What happens:
  1. Your ROM is read and the game's program is translated to C code on this computer.
  2. The game is compiled with the bundled compiler (LLVM/MinGW).
  3. SuperHangOn.exe is installed with your ROM(s) in the game folder.
